Comfort Gauge

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-30

Comfort Gauge ("the app") is published by 3Root. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Short version

The app collects no personal data. Nothing you enter leaves your device.

What the app stores

The app saves two preferences on your device: your temperature unit (°F or °C) and which view you last used (Map or Gauge). They stay on your device, are never sent anywhere, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not access the internet. The app has no network permission.
  • It requests no Android permissions: no location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage access.
  • It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or third-party SDKs.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.

Links

The app's info screen links to scientific sources on external websites. When you tap a link, it opens in your browser, and that website's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
