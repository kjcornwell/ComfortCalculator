Comfort Gauge

How comfortable is your air?

Comfort Gauge is an Android app. Set a temperature and a humidity, and it tells you how comfortable that air is for most people, and why. It judges two things separately: the temperature (using the ISO 7730 / ASHRAE 55 PMV-PPD model) and the humidity (using relative humidity and dew point).

  • Comfort map of temperature versus humidity. Drag anywhere on it to set both values.
  • A big "feels like" gauge, plus Heat Index, Apparent Temperature, Humidex and Dew Point gauges
  • Built-in guide to the science, with live charts and sources
  • °F or °C, fully offline, no ads, no accounts, no tracking
